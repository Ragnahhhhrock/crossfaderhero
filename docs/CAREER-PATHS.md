# Career paths

Every career starts in the same bedroom. The first big choice is which kind of DJ you want to be. There are two paths, each with its own gear, progression and ending.

| | **Pro DJ** | **Battle DJ** |
|---|---|---|
| Fantasy | Headline a major festival in a packed stadium | Be crowned the best DJ in the world |
| You start as | A bedroom DJ | A bedroom turntablist |
| Gear | Two CDJs + mixer (Phase 1 decks) | Two turntables + battle mixer (new) |
| Progress by | Gigs: crowds, bookings, reputation | Contests: local → national → world |
| Core skill | Reading the crowd, track selection, long blends | Scratching, beat juggling, tight routines |
| Win state | Headline set at a major festival | Win the World Final |

## 1. Pro DJ — bedroom to festival headliner

Climb the venue ladder by playing sets that keep the crowd moving. Each step is a bigger room, a longer set and a bigger fee.

| Stage | Venue | Crowd | Set |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bedroom (practice, streams, mixtapes) | 0 – online | any |
| 2 | House parties, bars | 20 – 100 | 1 h background / warm-up |
| 3 | Club warm-up slot | 200 – 500 | 1 – 2 h, don't outshine the headliner |
| 4 | Club peak-time / residency | 500 – 1,500 | 1.5 – 2 h |
| 5 | Headline club tours, small festival stages | 2,000 – 10,000 | 1.5 h |
| 6 | Main stage at major festivals | 20,000 – 80,000 (stadium) | 1 – 1.5 h headline |

- **Crowd energy** is the main measure: genre fit for the venue, energy curve, clean mixing, no dead air.
- **Bookings** come from reputation, followers (the SoundCloud-style platform) and, later, the manager.
- Bigger stages add pressure: tighter set times, bigger penalties for trainwrecks, production cues (pyro, lights) on drops.
- **Finale:** a headline set closing a major festival in front of a packed stadium.

## 2. Battle DJ — bedroom turntablist to world champion

Practise routines in the bedroom, then enter mixing contests and work up the ladder. Full rules, judging and rivals are in [COMPETITIONS.md](COMPETITIONS.md).

| Stage | Event | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bedroom practice | Learn scratches, juggles and routines; build a reputation online |
| 2 | Local contests | Enter, then start winning your city's battles |
| 3 | National championship | Win it to become national champion |
| 4 | International (world) championship | Win it — crowned the best DJ in the world |

- **Gear:** two turntables with control vinyl (playing the player's own MP3s, same as the CDJs) and a battle mixer with a fast cut-in crossfader.
- **Core techniques** to build: baby scratch, chirp, transformer, flare, crab; beat juggling (doubles, loops by hand); quick cuts and back-spins.
- **Training mode** in the bedroom: scratch drills with timing feedback, routine builder to plan and rehearse a contest set.
- **Finale:** win the World Final and lift the world championship trophy.

## Shared systems

- Same bedroom, music library (USB folders), track analysis, and SoundCloud-style platform for both paths.
- Money, reputation and followers work the same way; what earns them differs (gigs vs contests).
- The bedroom fills with each path's rewards: gig posters and festival passes, or contest trophies and battle records.
